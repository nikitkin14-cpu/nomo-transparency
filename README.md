# Nomo Messenger — Transparency Report

**Nomo** is a messenger built for freedom. No phone numbers. No censorship. No servers that can read your messages.

This repository exists to prove one thing: **we cannot spy on you, even if we wanted to.**

## 🧪 Verify, don't trust

We believe in **trust but verify**. Our cryptographic core will be open-source at [nomo-crypto-core](https://github.com/nikitkin14-cpu/nomo-crypto-core) (coming soon). You will be able to audit it yourself.

---

## 🛡️ Key principles

- **No phone number required.** You are not your SIM card.
- **End-to-end encrypted.** Messages are encrypted on your device, decrypted only on the recipient's device.
- **Decentralized future.** Mesh networks and P2P are on the roadmap. One day, Nomo will work even without internet.
- **No backdoors.** We don't have access to your chats. Period.
