# Privacy Overview

## What Nomo knows about you

**Nothing. Literally.**

- No phone number
- No email required
- No address book upload
- No real name needed

Your identity in Nomo is a cryptographic key pair. We don't know who you are.

---

## How messages are protected

1. **Authentication:** Ed25519 key pairs. Your private key never leaves your device.
2. **Encryption:** End-to-end. Messages are encrypted on your device, decrypted only on the recipient's device.
3. **Storage:** Messages exist on the server only until delivered. After delivery — deleted.
4. **Metadata:** We minimize metadata. No logs of who talks to whom.

---

## What we CANNOT do

- Read your messages
- See your contacts
- Track your location
- Hand over your data to anyone (because we don't have it)

---

## What happens if the server is seized?

Nothing. Encrypted messages are useless without your keys. And your keys are on your device, not on the server.

---

## Future: Mesh networks

When P2P and mesh networks are implemented, messages won't touch any server at all. Direct device-to-device.
